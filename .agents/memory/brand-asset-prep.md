---
name: Brand asset preparation
description: Supplied brand marks may be square raster canvases rather than ready-to-place transparent logos.
---

Treat supplied brand marks as source artwork: inspect the visible bounds and prepare a cropped transparent derivative before placing it in navigation.

**Why:** A full-canvas logo can render as an unexpected white block even when the mark itself is correct, which damages the first impression of a brand site.

**How to apply:** When a logo arrives as a raster image, preserve the original as source material and use a derived cropped asset for UI placement.
